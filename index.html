About

I'm a Sydney-based designer who has worked across advertising, print production and digital content. I started in advertising agencies in Venezuela, then worked in design, pre-press and web systems in Sydney, before becoming Digital Content Coordinator at XCOLLECTIVE (formerly Technology 360 Group). There I looked after six brands, designing everything from trade show stands and flyers to eDMs, screen animations and websites.

I’m an independent worker who thrives as part of a team, open to feedback and always up for a challenge. Systematic and conscientious but flexible enough to adapt, improve and change according to the demands of the job. Colleagues relied on me to make things look right: templates people actually use, documents that look professional, and brands that stay consistent wherever they appear. I hold a Master of Design Science (Digital Media) from the University of Sydney, and I work in English and Spanish.

Print and events, APP and ILG 2025

Trade show campaigns for Future Shop and Technology FX

Complete stand and handout material for two industry conferences, one for pharmacy and one for independent liquor retailers, carried from 3x3 pop-up backdrops down to A5 flyers.

Client
Future Shop and Technology FX (Technology 360 Group)
Events
APP (Australasian Pharmacy Professional Conference & Trade Exhibition) and the ILG (Independent Liquor Group) Conference
My role
Sole designer, working from supplied copy; generated and selected the AI imagery
Deliverables
3x3 pop-up stand backdrops, pull-up banners, A4 and A5 flyers

The brief

Future Shop and Technology FX needed marketing material to promote their retail technology and IT services at two conferences: one for pharmacy professionals and one for independent liquor retailers.

What I did

  • Designed every piece of both stands and their handouts, from A5 flyers to the 3x3 pop-up backdrops.
  • Built one visual system per brand and adapted it for each audience. Future Shop's flyers exist in pharmacy, liquor, grocery and general versions.
  • Kept the backdrops, banners and flyers consistent, so the brand read the same from across the hall as it did in a visitor's hand.
  • Created the Future Shop imagery of pharmacists and liquor-store owners with AI: generated many variations for each piece and selected the most convincing, at a time when AI images often had visible flaws.

Outcome

Both stands were built and used at the events. Internal and external feedback on the material was very positive, and both events generated leads for the business.

Imagery: AI-generated, selected by me (Future Shop); licensed stock (Technology FX). Faces in event photos are blurred.

Screens, motion and print, APP 2026

XPLAY to REXIMO:
Launching a retail media network,
then rebranding it

Launch material for the group's new retail media network at APP 2026, then carried across its rebrand from XPLAY to REXIMO, in print, on screen and online.

Client
XCOLLECTIVE (formerly Technology 360 Group)
Event
APP 2026, Gold Coast Convention and Exhibition Centre, 12–14 March
My role
Designer and motion designer; applied the existing XPLAY and REXIMO logos
Deliverables
Screen animation, animated screen ads, pull-up banner, flyer, sizzle reel

The brief

Launch the group's retail media network to pharmacy owners at APP 2026, then move the campaign to the new REXIMO name after the rebrand.

What I did

  • Animated a 30-second kinetic-type loop for the stand's ultra-wide 23.1-inch screens (1920 × 158 px), carrying the campaign line "Unleash the power of the shelf".
  • Animated 15-second ads for Colgate, QV and Herbs of Gold in the same format, playing on the shelf-edge screens; Hydralyte's was supplied by the client.
  • Designed the XPLAY pull-up banner and two-sided flyer for the stand, and supplied the logo artwork for the stand builder's wall panels, reviewing their layout drafts.
  • After the rebrand, carried the flyer across to REXIMO.
  • Made a 40-second sizzle reel from scratch in After Effects to promote REXIMO.

Outcome

The XPLAY material was used on the stand at APP 2026, and the REXIMO material carried the network forward after the rebrand.

Shelf-edge screen animations

Made for 23.1-inch screens just 158 pixels tall, so each message had to work in one line.

Logo and web, 2026

DistributionX and REXIMO:
A logo redesign and two websites

Two company websites taken from a basic HTML structure to finished, on-brand sites, plus an approved redesign of the DistributionX logo that brings it into the group's "X" brand family.

Client
XCOLLECTIVE (formerly Technology 360 Group)
Live sites
distributionx.com.au and reximo.media
My role
Sole designer and builder (with AI assistance), working from a supplied HTML structure and copy, originally created with AI assistance by the company's Managing Director
Deliverables
DistributionX logo redesign, two websites

The brief

Turn two working but unbranded website structures into finished sites that look like part of the same group of companies.

What I did

  • Suggested redesigning the DistributionX logo to match the group's other brands that share the same "X" brand mark (XCOLLECTIVE and REXIMO): bold capitals and an "X" built from two motion-faded, opposing arrow shapes, while keeping DistributionX's own personality (its distinctive font and colours). The idea was approved, and I redesigned the logo.
  • DistributionX site: replaced the grey image placeholders with real images in every product card, added an image behind the opening headline, and applied the new logo throughout.
  • REXIMO site: kept the supplied layout and wording, and replaced the empty "Image placeholder" hero and text-only cards with full-width in-store photography, image-topped cards and the REXIMO purple.
  • Built both with AI assistance, which let one person deliver two finished sites.

Outcome

Both sites are live, and the new DistributionX logo is in use on its site.
